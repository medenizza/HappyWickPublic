BTC MVRV Ratio

MVRV compares Bitcoin's market cap to its realized cap — what holders actually paid for their coins. Below 1.0 signals holders are underwater (historically a buy zone), 1.0–2.0 is fair value, 2.0–3.5 is overvalued, and above 3.5 has historically marked market tops.

DXY — US Dollar Index

The ICE U.S. Dollar Index tracks dollar strength against a basket of major currencies (euro, yen, pound and more). It moves opposite to crypto — a strengthening dollar tends to pull money out of Bitcoin, while a weakening dollar often fuels rallies.

US 10-Year Yield

The US 10-Year Treasury Yield is the benchmark interest rate on US government bonds. Rising yields make "safe" bonds more attractive and pull money away from risk assets like Bitcoin; falling yields tend to push investors back toward crypto.
