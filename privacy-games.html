HappyWick: Mini Games

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

No data collected

Overview

HappyWick: Mini Games is a free offline arcade game. We do not collect, store, share, or transmit any personal data. This policy explains exactly what that means in practice.

What we do not collect

Personal information

No name, email, phone number, or any other identifying information is requested or stored.

Device data

We do not read your device ID, advertising ID, or any hardware identifiers.

Location

The app does not access or request your location at any time.

Usage analytics

No analytics, crash reporting, or event tracking of any kind is implemented in this app.

Internet access

The game runs fully offline. No network requests are made during gameplay.

Local storage

The app may save your high scores and game settings locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is only used to improve your in-game experience.

In-app purchases & ads

HappyWick: Mini Games contains no third-party advertisements and no in-app purchases. The game includes a link to happywick.app, our own website. The game is completely free.

Virtual coins disclaimer

All coins, tokens, and rewards visible within the game are entirely virtual. They have no monetary value and do not represent real cryptocurrency of any kind. This app is not a financial product and does not facilitate any cryptocurrency transactions.

Children

Because we collect no personal data whatsoever, this app is safe for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect information from children or any other users.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, we will update the date above. We will not introduce data collection without updating this document and resubmitting the app to Google Play.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us at happywick.app.
