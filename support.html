Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a Bitcoin or EVM wallet?
Go to the Wallets tab, choose the BTC or Ethereum sub-tab, tap the + button, and paste your wallet address. EVM wallets are also tracked across other supported chains (Arbitrum, Base, Optimism, Polygon and more). Happy Wick will monitor it for new transactions.
Why am I not receiving push notifications?
Make sure notifications are enabled in your iPhone Settings → Happy Wick → Notifications. Also check that Daily Briefing is turned on in the app's Settings tab.
How often is the market data updated?
Market data is refreshed every minute via our server. Pull down on any screen to force a refresh.
How do I delete my account?
Go to Settings → Delete My Account. This will permanently delete all your data including saved wallets and preferences.
Is Happy Wick financial advice?
No. Happy Wick is a read-only market data app. Nothing in the app constitutes financial advice. Always do your own research before making investment decisions.
