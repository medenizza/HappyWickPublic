# Happy Wick

Source for the Happy Wick marketing website — https://happywick.app

## Products

**Happy Wick Trade** — https://trade.happywick.app
Non-custodial spot trading web app. Connect a wallet and trade with market, limit, stop-loss and take-profit orders across 13 EVM networks, including BNB Chain. No sign-up; funds stay in the user's wallet. Execution powered by Definitive's Flash API.

**Happy Wick mobile app** — iOS and Android
Free, read-only crypto tracker: portfolio tracking via public wallet addresses, Fear & Greed Index, BTC dominance and AI market summaries.

- App Store: https://apps.apple.com/app/id6763779729
- Google Play: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.darko.medenica.happywick
